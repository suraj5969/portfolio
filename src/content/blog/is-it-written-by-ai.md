## Are my blogs written by AI?

**Yes**, partially written by AI. Writing a blog is a skill and it actually takes time to write a good blog. And unfortunately, I have never practiced that skill and I'm not good at that. And don't want to either spend much time on writing a blog that could be done by with help of AI.

I give a brief to AI with speech to text about what I need to write, what things I did, what idea I want to convey, what are my results, etc and ask to generate a rough draft of the blog.

 Then I read it, edit it, add my preferences, make a final draft version, again give to AI for grammar check, remove unnecessary words or paragraphs, see if the idea conveys clearly and then publish.
